In recent years, the development and application of deep learning and artificial intelligence have left a profound impression on the world. At the heart of these technologies lie neural networks—yet very few truly understand how they work.

This book, written in a light and approachable tone, gradually unveils the mathematical ideas behind neural networks and shows how to develop them using the Python programming language. It takes readers on a delightful yet structured journey—starting from a simple idea and building step by step toward a comprehensive understanding of how neural networks function.

No advanced math is required beyond the high school level, and the book even provides an accessible introduction to calculus. Its goal is to make neural networks understandable to as many people as possible.

Readers will learn how to build their own neural networks in Python, train them to recognize handwritten digits, and achieve results comparable to professional-grade neural networks.

This book is ideal for anyone interested in deep learning, AI, and neural networks—especially those looking to explore neural network development through Python programming.

It is a carefully crafted beginner-friendly guide that helps readers build a real, functional neural network without requiring complex mathematics or deep theoretical knowledge.
