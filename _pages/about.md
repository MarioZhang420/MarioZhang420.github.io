Hi there! I’m a junior studying Computer Science (B.S.) at UW–Madison. I’m all about back-end development and exploring how AI can transform real-world applications—like e-commerce, search, and automation. Recently, I’ve been experimenting with cloud deployment and full-stack work, building an OpenAI-based PDF Q&A tool and a Spring Boot ordering system on AWS, while also diving into Langchain, React, and GCP.

I’m also drawn to quantitative analysis, sparked by high school business simulation competitions and the L’Oréal Brandstorm challenge in freshman year. Looking ahead, I plan to double-major in Mathematics to deepen my technical foundation and expand the ways I can tackle complex challenges.

I’m all about how computer science can make everyday life easier—whether it’s helping seniors get the support they need, improving Google Maps, or powering ride-hailing and food delivery apps. It still amazes me that even a tiny feature can reach countless people and shape their daily routines. That’s what really fuels my passion: the chance to build tech that genuinely helps others and brings our world a little closer together.
