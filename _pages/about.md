Hi there! I'm a Computer Science senior at the **University of Wisconsin–Madison**, graduating in **December 2026**, and I spent Fall 2025 as a visiting student at **UC Berkeley**. I'm interested in backend engineering, data infrastructure, distributed systems, and AI/ML systems.

I've interned at **The Trade Desk**, **Tencent America**, and **Alibaba**, working across large-scale data infrastructure, ML experiment automation, RAG/agentic AI, and full-stack development:

- **The Trade Desk** — built a dataset backfill and experimentation platform on Scala, Spark, Airflow, and MLflow that turns Airflow DAGs into self-service backfills, now widely used across the KPI model team.
- **Tencent America** — built internal RAG and multi-agent applications over 10K+ documents, including a GraphRAG system for incident root-cause analysis.
- **Alibaba** — modernized a legacy mail-dispatch module in React and TypeScript, cutting bug reports by 40% and first-paint time by 30%.

Before that, I worked on computer vision for industrial welding robots, and co-authored a paper on interpretable incremental learning for blood cell image recognition.

I've previously received offers from Microsoft, The Trade Desk, WeRide, Bosch, Capital One, and Amazon Robotics, and reached team matching at Google and Qualcomm. **I'm currently exploring 2027 new grad software engineering opportunities** — feel free to reach out!

I'm all about how computer science can make everyday life easier — whether it's helping seniors get the support they need, improving Google Maps, or powering ride-hailing and food delivery apps. It still amazes me that even a tiny feature can reach countless people and shape their daily routines. That's what really fuels my passion: the chance to build tech that genuinely helps others and brings our world a little closer together.
